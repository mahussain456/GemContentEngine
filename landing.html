The quality bar

Six rules every drop must actually clear.

A lot of AI tools just produce average content faster. GEM is wired to refuse the obvious filler. Each agent's output gets graded against the rules below before the next agent runs.

i.
One clear idea per post
If the post tries to say three things, it says nothing. Architect picks one angle and kills the rest.
ii.
A two-second hook
Curiosity, tension, or a contrarian frame in the first line. No "in today's video," no warm-up filler.
iii.
Specific examples, not generic advice
Real numbers, real workflows, real tools. Writer rejects its own draft when it goes vague.
iv.
Visuals that match the brand
Brand voice flows into every Midjourney, DALL-E, and FLUX prompt, so you don't get random AI art slop.
v.
A CTA tied to the business goal
Not "follow for more". Save, share, reply, comment, or click, picked per channel and per intent.
vi.
A human approval beat
Pipeline output is a draft, not a publish. You always read and edit before anything ships.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about GEM Content Engine

Do I need to install anything?
No. GEM is a single HTML file that you open in any modern browser. The one thing you might want to install is Ollama, which lets you run the AI locally for free. Otherwise, paste an OpenAI, Anthropic, or OpenRouter key into Settings and you're running.
What's the cost per generation?
Ollama costs you nothing. OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini runs about two to five cents per full pipeline (that's six agents and roughly thirty LLM calls). Claude Sonnet sits around twelve cents. For comparison, Jasper bills you $49 a month whether you log in or not.
Is my data private?
In Ollama mode, your data stays on your machine. In cloud mode, your prompts go straight to whichever provider you pick (OpenAI, Anthropic, or OpenRouter), exactly the same as using ChatGPT directly. GEM has no server of its own. We never see what you generate.
Can I use it for client work?
Yes. Founder's Edition includes a commercial-use license, so you can use the output for your clients, your own brand, or your own products. White-label rebranding is on the roadmap for higher tiers once GEM ships its team features.
How does Scout pull trends, and where does it fall short?
Scout reads live signals from Reddit and HackerNews directly in your browser, since those services are CORS-friendly. Google Trends is stricter about CORS, so by default Scout falls back to r/popular as a trending proxy. That covers about 95% of niches well. If you want the full Google Trends feed, the User Manual walks you through a one-line local proxy. The point is, you always know exactly what Scout is reading.

The bigger modules I'm working on are a daily auto-brief tailored to your niche, an approve / edit / regenerate / reject workflow with version history, direct publishing to Buffer, Later, and Meta, and a performance loop where yesterday's winners feed tomorrow's brief. Because lifetime updates are part of the license, every release lands at no extra cost.
What's the difference vs. ChatGPT alone?
ChatGPT is a generalist that hands you a blank prompt. GEM is six specialist agents with locked schemas (Brand, Scout, Architect, Writer, Visual Director, and Channel Adapter), each with its own system prompt, output validation, and handoff to the next agent. You get a finished content pack in about ninety seconds instead of a long thread of follow-up prompts.

Does GEM work with Claude or other Anthropic models?
Yes. GEM supports Anthropic's Claude through the Anthropic API. Paste your API key (it starts with sk-ant-) into Settings, select Claude as the provider, and you're running. The Writer and Visual Director agents work particularly well with Claude's longer context window. A full pipeline run on Claude Sonnet costs roughly twelve cents.
Can I use GEM without internet access?
Yes, completely. Install Ollama locally on your machine, pull a model like qwen2.5:14b, and GEM runs entirely offline. Your prompts and generated content never touch the internet in Ollama mode. The only thing that needs internet is the initial download of gem.html and the Ollama installer.
Is GEM beginner-friendly?
Yes. The thirty-second on-ramp is in the WELCOME.md file inside your buyer ZIP. Open gem.html, paste your provider key (or point at local Ollama), pick a niche from the twelve quick-start templates, and click Generate. Most buyers ship their first finished content pack within ten minutes of downloading.
How long does it take to generate one content pack?
About ninety seconds end to end. Six agents run in sequence: Brand voice (10 seconds), Scout trends (15 seconds), Architect structure (15 seconds), Writer script (20 seconds), Visual prompts (25 seconds), and Channel Adapter (15 seconds). Times vary based on your LLM provider and connection speed.

Is GEM open source?
No. GEM Content Engine is proprietary commercial software with a one-time purchase license. The single HTML file ships unminified, so you can read every line of the code if you want to. You can also modify it for your own internal use under the license terms documented in Terms of Service.
Can GEM generate full videos?
GEM writes the video prompts (Kling AI, RunwayML, scene-by-scene) but doesn't generate the video itself. You paste those prompts into your preferred video AI to produce the final clips. This keeps GEM free of recurring video-generation API costs and lets you pick whichever video model fits your budget.
