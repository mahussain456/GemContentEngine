About · v1.7

Built by an operator,
for operators.

GEM Content Engine is not a venture-backed AI startup. It's a focused tool, built by someone who runs real content channels every day, sold for nine dollars to the first hundred buyers, then ratcheted up as the cohort grows.

Why GEM exists

The AI content market has converged on a single product: a chat box wrapped in a $49/month subscription. You pay forever, your data trains someone else's model, and the output is indistinguishable from a thousand other creators using the same prompts.

GEM flips that. One purchase. Six specialist agents. No server. The product runs in your browser. You can keep it local on Ollama for free, or point it at any cloud LLM using your own key. Your prompts and outputs never go through our infrastructure, because we don't have any.

That architectural choice is the product. It's also the reason the price ladder can stay honest. With no servers to feed, we don't need recurring revenue to break even.

Who builds this

GEM is built by the operator behind two live content channels: AI Decoded and Tools That Work, covering AI for creators and operator-grade productivity tools respectively. Every release of GEM gets dogfooded on those channels for at least thirty days before it ships to buyers. The Quality Bar checks in v1.7 exist because they had to clear those channels first.

"If a feature in GEM doesn't survive a month of real publishing on AI Decoded and Tools That Work, it doesn't ship. A lot of AI tools just produce average content faster. GEM was built to refuse that." Founder, GEM Content Engine

What GEM is not

Not a chat wrapper

Six specialist agents with locked schemas, validated handoff between each, and a Quality Bar gate. Not a $9 ChatGPT skin.

Not a SaaS

No server, no database, no account. We have no way to read your work. The product file is yours forever.

Not a daily auto-brief, yet

That's on the roadmap. v1.7 is on-demand generation. Founder's Edition buyers vote on what ships next.

Not an enterprise sale

Studio tier caps at $149. White-label and team seats are future tiers, not today's product.

The principles we actually hold

  • Privacy by architecture. Your data can't leak because we never receive it.
  • One purchase, lifetime updates. Within the v1.x major version, every release is free.
  • Founders shape the roadmap. The first 100 buyers vote on what ships next.
  • Quality Bar over volume. Six rules every drop must clear before publish. A lot of AI tools just produce average content faster. GEM is wired to refuse.
  • Honest disclosures. The live FAQ documents the Trending Mode Google Trends CORS fallback. No marketing magic.
